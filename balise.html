Balise — Privacy policy

Balise · io.github.bdherouville.balise · a trail companion for Wear OS watches

Effective 2026-08-08 · last updated 2026-08-08

Balise records GPS tracks and follows routes you load yourself, using map data stored on the watch. This policy covers the Balise app distributed on Google Play. It is the only privacy policy for that app, and it applies to every user, everywhere.

Your location never leaves your watch. Balise has no account, no login, no ads, and no companion phone app. It works fully offline.

Who is responsible for your data

Bertrand d'Hérouville, publisher of Balise, is the data controller.
Privacy contact: balise-privacy@probos.fr

What Balise stores on your watch

All of it stays in the app's private storage on the watch. None of it is uploaded, and Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app, so it is not copied off the device that way either.

  • Recorded tracks — GPS positions, timestamps and derived session data for trips you record.
  • Routes — GPX files you import, and their geometry.
  • Offline map packages — map data you choose to download.
  • Your settings, including whether you have opted in to diagnostics.

Location

Balise requests precise and approximate location, and runs a foreground service while recording so a trip is not lost when the screen turns off.

Your location is used only on the watch. It is never transmitted anywhere. No code path in Balise sends a coordinate off the device, and no diagnostics event can carry one — the diagnostics described below can only send values from a fixed, reviewed list of event names.

Balise does record your location locally, which is the whole point of the app, and you can delete it at any time.

When Balise uses the network

Balise makes no network connection during a trip. Exactly two things can reach the internet, both outside of recording:

  1. Downloading offline maps — only when you explicitly ask, over HTTPS, from the map catalogue the app is configured with. Map data is signed and verified before installation.
  2. Optional anonymous diagnostics — off by default. See below.

Nothing else in the app opens a connection.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default and only run if you explicitly turn them on in Settings → Privacy & data.

  • Only pseudonymous event names from a fixed, reviewed list are sent — never location, route names, file names, screen contents, free text, error text, or anything identifying you.
  • Data goes to PostHog, hosted in the EU, over HTTPS.
  • Capture is suspended entirely while a trip is active, paused or in an uncertain state. Diagnostics never run alongside a recording.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time; collection stops immediately.

Processor: PostHog Inc., EU-hosted infrastructure.
Legal basis (GDPR): your consent, given by turning the setting on.
Retention: 30 days from ingestion, after which events are deleted by the processor.
Processor contact for retention or deletion requests: privacy@posthog.com

If diagnostics are left off, Balise makes no analytics connection at all.

Deleting your data

Because Balise has no account, there is no server-side profile to erase, and no request form to fill in — deletion is something you perform directly:

  • Recordings can be deleted individually in the app. A recording belonging to an active trip is protected until the trip ends.
  • Offline map packages can be deleted individually. The map currently in use is protected.
  • Everything at once is removed by uninstalling Balise, or by clearing its app data in the watch's system settings. This also removes imported routes, which cannot yet be deleted individually.
  • Diagnostics events already sent, if you had opted in, expire after 30 days. To have them erased sooner, write to balise-privacy@probos.fr and we will pass the request to the processor.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to complain to your national supervisory authority. In practice most of these are moot: the app holds your data on your own device and we hold nothing that identifies you. Write to balise-privacy@probos.fr to exercise any of them.

What Balise never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDK is present.
  • No account, sign-in or user identifier.
  • No access to contacts, calendar, photos, files, health or fitness data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, and no transfer for advertising or brokerage.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No automatic capture of screens, errors, crashes or your identity.

Children

Balise is not directed at children, is not listed in a children's category, and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Every network connection Balise makes uses HTTPS, and downloaded map packages are cryptographically signed and verified before they are installed.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date. Because Balise's Data Safety declaration on Google Play must match this policy, both are updated together.
